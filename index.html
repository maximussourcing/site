How we work

One partner from specification to switch-on.

Whether you need a single breaker or a 25 MW AI campus, one team manages the process from requirement to handover.

  1. Define

    We turn your site, load and schedule into a clear technical specification.

  2. Source

    We tender to qualified manufacturers and compare offers on price, lead time and compliance.

  3. Verify

    Factory acceptance testing and inspection happen before anything ships.

  4. Deliver

    We plan sea and road freight, customs and route surveys for oversized loads.

  5. Commission

    We coordinate installation, integrated testing and handover on site.

Shorter lead times

Domestic lead times for switchgear, large transformers and big generators now often run past a year. We find capacity in manufacturer slots and stock to keep your schedule.

Established manufacturers

We partner with large producers whose equipment runs on hundreds of gigawatts of installed capacity worldwide, including exports to dozens of countries.

Power and compute together

Because we source both the generation equipment and the data center, on-site power, cooling and IT are designed to work as one system.
