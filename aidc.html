Compute Service Agreements

We bring together GPU capacity owners, compute buyers, facility providers and capital, and help structure the compute service agreement (CSA) that links them.

  • Matching AI compute demand with capacity on firm power
  • Capacity tranches, term, service levels and delivery milestones
  • Server delivery schedules tied to facility and power readiness
  • Coordination with lenders, equipment vendors and operators

Modular Solution Sourcing

A modular AI data center turns a construction project into an industrial product. IT, power, cooling and fire protection are built as standard modules in parallel, tested at the factory, then assembled on site.

  • Competitive tendering among qualified module manufacturers
  • Co-design of layout, redundancy level and cooling approach
  • Factory acceptance testing, so modules arrive proven
  • Sea and road logistics, including route surveys for oversized loads

Inside the modules

Standard 20, 40 and 45 ft ISO container formats, built for ocean freight and road transport. They combine into layouts from a single edge site to a multi-megawatt campus.
